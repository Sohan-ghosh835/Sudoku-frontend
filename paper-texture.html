A page on paper

Built entirely from CSS and SVG filters, no image files. Two grain passes (fine and coarse fractal noise) give it a fibrous surface, faint laid and chain lines nod to handmade paper, fiber streaks run in two directions, blotches add uneven foxing, and a vignette plus inset shadow deckle the edges.

Reuse it by copying the .paper-bg class — including its ::before and ::after pseudo-elements — into your stylesheet and applying it to any container.
